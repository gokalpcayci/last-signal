# Official challenge resources

Source: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/nasas-discarded-equipment-storytelling/?tab=resources

- **NSSDCA** (master catalog — mission parameters, final resting place, status): https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/

## Moon (Phase 1)
- Apollo Lunar Modules (descent stages left as launch pads): https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1969-059C
- Lunar Roving Vehicles (3 moon buggies, Apollo 15/16/17): https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1971-063C
- ALSEP science packages (seismometers, retroreflectors…): https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1969-099C
- Surveyor landers (1960s soft-landing probes): https://science.nasa.gov/mission/surveyor-3/

## Mars (Phase 2)
- Sojourner / Pathfinder: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-pathfinder/
- Spirit & Opportunity: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-exploration-rovers-spirit-and-opportunity/
- Viking 1 & 2: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/viking-1/
- Phoenix & InSight: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/insight/
- Ingenuity: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/ingenuity-mars-helicopter/

## Deep space / other bodies (Phase 3)
- Dawn (orbiting Ceres): https://science.nasa.gov/mission/dawn/
- NEAR Shoemaker (on asteroid Eros): https://science.nasa.gov/mission/near-shoemaker/
- Pioneer 10 & 11: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/pioneer-10/
- Kepler (Earth-trailing orbit): https://science.nasa.gov/mission/kepler/
